`checkXml` and `formatXml` check and format a theme as Blogger was found to save and render it. What Blogger refuses, or fails on when rendering, is an error: a section id blank, with spaces around it or the same as another once its entities are decoded, `expr:id` on a section, a widget id over `HTML1000`, a second widget of a type a blog has one of, a section in `<head>` or in a widget, a skin in `<body>`, `<b:template-skin>` without `b:layoutsVersion`, an includable id twice in a widget, as XML reads ids, a `b:defaultmarkup` type it does not have, a `<b:attr>`, `<b:class>` or `<b:tag>` name it can not render, more than 40 `<b:with>` and `<b:loop>` one in another. What it removes when saving, or does not render, is a warning, so it can be removed: comments in sections and widgets and processing instructions, what is not a widget in a section, the attributes it removes from a widget, what is in `<b:else>`, `<b:default>`, `<b:case>`, `<b:elseif>`, `<b:eval>`, `<b:attr>`, `<b:class>`, `<b:param>` or a data tag, a branch after the `<b:else>` or `<b:default>` that ends it, two includables of a widget of which Blogger keeps one, their ids the same once trimmed. What Blogger accepts is accepted, as the root attributes, the XML declaration and the doctype it rewrites, an unknown `b:` element out of widgets, two template skins, `expr:var` and `expr:index` on `<b:loop>`, which win over `var` and `index`, and the Theme Designer variables it reads, only `<Group>` and `<Variable>` with their values in double quotes. The defaults of color, length and background variables are checked as Blogger checks them. Formatting writes what Blogger saves: values decoded and escaped as it escapes them, expressions printed on one line by `blogger-expr`, which Blogger saves as they are written, text with `&`, `<`, `>`, `"` and `'` escaped and the characters past ASCII that are not letters or digits as decimal references, in comments too, settings trimmed and in a CDATA section when they have markup, the declarations without a doctype added, the children of sections and widgets ordered and indented and their end tags on their own lines, the attributes of widgets added and removed, `expr:` ones too, empty skins as an empty CDATA section, and a `value` added to each `<Variable>` without one

`blogger-hbs validate <file>` validates an XML theme, and `blogger-hbs format <file>` formats it, written to stdout or with `-o, --out <file>` to a file, as a build does with the rendered theme. With `--json`, the result is printed as JSON: the warnings and info of a valid theme, with the formatted theme or where it is written, or every error

`--version` shows the copyright, the license, the URL of the project, and the version of Node.js and the platform

The `Header` widget, "Page Header" in the Layout editor, prints the title of the blog, its description, and optionally a banner image. The banner can sit behind the text, take the place of the title and description, or come before the description. The title is the blog's own: changing it in the widget changes **Settings > General > Title**, and the other way round.

## Quick example

The title is an `h1` on list pages and an `h2` on post pages, where the post title is the `h1`. It links to the homepage, except on the homepage itself. A banner, when one is set, is printed as an image above the title.

<CodeTabs>

```xml title="theme.xml" {2,28}
<b:section id='theme_section_header' maxwidgets='1' name='Header' showaddelement='false'>
  <b:widget id='Header1' locked='true' title='My Blog (Header)' type='Header' version='2' visible='true'>
    <b:widget-settings>
      <b:widget-setting name='displayHeight'>0</b:widget-setting>
      <b:widget-setting name='displayUrl'/>
      <b:widget-setting name='displayWidth'>0</b:widget-setting>
      <b:widget-setting name='imagePlacement'>BEHIND</b:widget-setting>
      <b:widget-setting name='sectionWidth'>-1</b:widget-setting>
      <b:widget-setting name='shrinkToFit'>false</b:widget-setting>
      <b:widget-setting name='useImage'>false</b:widget-setting>
    </b:widget-settings>
    <b:includable id='main' var='this'>
      <b:class expr:name='data:sourceUrl ? "has-banner" : "no-banner"'/>
      <b:include cond='data:sourceUrl' name='image'/>
      <b:include name='title'/>
      <b:include cond='data:description' name='description'/>
    </b:includable>
    <b:includable id='image'>
      <b:tag class='brand-logo' expr:name='data:view.isHomepage ? "div" : "a"'>
        <b:attr cond='not data:view.isHomepage' expr:value='data:blog.homepageUrl.canonical' name='href'/>
        <img expr:alt='data:title' expr:height='data:height' expr:src='data:sourceUrl' expr:width='data:width'/>
      </b:tag>
    </b:includable>
    <b:includable id='title'>
      <b:tag class='brand-title' expr:name='data:view.isSingleItem ? "h2" : "h1"'>
        <b:tag expr:name='data:view.isHomepage ? "span" : "a"'>
          <b:attr cond='not data:view.isHomepage' expr:value='data:blog.homepageUrl.canonical' name='href'/>
          <data:title/>
        </b:tag>
      </b:tag>
    </b:includable>
    <b:includable id='description'>
      <p class='brand-description'><data:description/></p>
    </b:includable>
  </b:widget>
</b:section>
```

```html title="Rendered, on a post page, without a banner"
<div class='widget Header no-banner' data-version='2' id='Header1'>
  <h2 class='brand-title'><a href='https://example.blogspot.com/'>My Blog</a></h2>
  <p class='brand-description'>Notes on building Blogger themes.</p>
</div>
```

</CodeTabs>

The `title` attribute of `<b:widget>` is the name of the widget in the Layout editor, by default the blog title followed by `(Header)`. What the page shows is `data:title`.

|                     |                                                                                                                         | Source                            |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |

## Widget settings

Blogger writes them when the widget is saved in the Layout editor. See [Widgets](../layout/widgets.mdx) for how settings are stored.

| Setting          | Values                                    | Without a banner | Data                 | Meaning                                                     | Source                            |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| `displayUrl`     | image URL, or empty                       | empty            | `sourceUrl`, `image` | The banner                                                  | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `displayWidth`   | number                                    | `0`              | `width`              | Width of the banner                                         | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `displayHeight`  | number                                    | `0`              | `height`             | Height of the banner                                        | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `useImage`       | `true`, `false`                           | `false`          | `useImage`           | The header has a banner                                     | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `shrinkToFit`    | `true`, `false`                           | `false`          | `shrinkToFit`        | "Shrink to fit": scale the banner down to the section width | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `imagePlacement` | `BEHIND`, `REPLACE`, `BEFORE_DESCRIPTION` | `BEHIND`         | `imagePlacement`     | Where the banner goes                                       | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `sectionWidth`   | number                                    | `-1`             | `sectionWidth`       | Width of the section when the banner was saved. Legacy      | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |

When the theme is saved: <BloggerSource kind="observed" />

- A Header written with no settings gets the values of the "Without a banner" column, and the title `BLOG (Header)`, where `BLOG` is the blog title.
- With a `displayUrl`, `displayWidth` must be 1 or more: `0` is refused "Must be at least 1". A `displayUrl` that is not a URL is refused "URL is malformed".
- Without a `displayUrl`, `useImage`, `displayWidth` and `imagePlacement` are reset to their defaults: `REPLACE` and `BEFORE_DESCRIPTION` are stored `BEHIND`, and `displayWidth` `abc` or `-1` is stored `0`, without a message.
- `imagePlacement` is case-sensitive: `behind` is refused.
- An unknown setting name is refused with "The widget settings in widget with id Header1 is not valid.", without the name.

## Widget data

Google documents `title` and `description`. The other fields are read by Blogger's own markup.

| Field                        | V1  | V2  | Type                     | Holds                                                                                                         | Source                            |
| ---------------------------- | --- | --- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| `title`                      | ✓   | ✓   | string                   | The blog title, up to 100 characters                                                                          | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `description`                | ✓   | ✓   | string                   | The blog description, up to 500 characters                                                                    | <BloggerSource kind="official" /> |
| `useImage`                   | ✓   | ✓   | boolean                  | `true` when `displayUrl` holds a URL                                                                          | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `imagePlacement`             | ✓   | ✓   | string                   | `BEHIND`, `REPLACE` or `BEFORE_DESCRIPTION`, see [Placement of the banner](#placement-of-the-banner)          | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `sourceUrl`                  | ✓   | ✓   | string                   | The banner URL, as a plain string                                                                             | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `image`                      |     | ✓   | image                    | The banner at its original size, for `responsiveImage` and `resizeImage`                                      | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `width`                      | ✓   | ✓   | string (V1), number (V2) | Display width of the banner                                                                                   | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `height`                     | ✓   | ✓   | string (V1), number (V2) | Display height of the banner                                                                                  | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `widthStyleStr`              | ✓   |     | string                   | A declaration `width: Npx;`, the original width or, with shrink to fit, the section width                     | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `backgroundPositionStyleStr` | ✓   | ✓   | string                   | `left` or `right`, from the writing direction of the blog language, like `data:blog.locale.languageAlignment` | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `shrinkToFit`                | ✓   | ✓   | boolean                  | The "Shrink to fit" option                                                                                    | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `sectionWidth`               | ✓   | ✓   | number                   | The section width saved with the banner, `-1` without one. Refreshed only when the widget is saved again      | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `mobile`                     | ✓   |     | boolean                  | `true` in the classic mobile view, `?m=1`. Use `data:view.isMobile` instead                                   | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |

The 300-pixel cap of the [Image](./image.mdx) widget does not apply: header banners are usually wider. <BloggerSource kind="observed" />

### Placement of the banner

| `imagePlacement`     | Option in the Layout editor             | What Blogger's markup prints                                           |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `BEHIND`             | Behind title and description            | The title and the description, with the banner as their CSS background |
| `REPLACE`            | Instead of title and description        | The banner as an `img`, linked to the homepage                         |
| `BEFORE_DESCRIPTION` | Have description placed after the image | The banner as an `img`, then the description                           |

With `REPLACE`, the title is not printed by the built-in markup of version 1, which leaves the page without the blog name as text. A theme that keeps the banner and the title, hiding the title with CSS, keeps it readable for search engines and screen readers. A theme can also ignore the setting and decide from `data:sourceUrl` alone, as the first example does.

## Built-in includables

| Includable         | V1  | V2              | Renders                                                                | Source                            |
| ------------------ | --- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| `main`             | ✓   | ✓, `var='this'` | Picks the banner, title and description from `imagePlacement`          | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `title`            | ✓   | ✓               | The blog title, linked to the homepage except on the homepage          | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `description`      | ✓   | ✓               | The description                                                        | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `image`            |     | ✓               | The banner as a responsive image, linked to the homepage               | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |
| `behindImageStyle` |     | ✓               | A `<style>` that sets the banner as the background of `.header-widget` | <BloggerSource kind="observed" /> |

### `main`

**Version 2.** `main` wraps everything in `div.header-widget`. It includes `image` for `REPLACE` and `BEFORE_DESCRIPTION`, `title` for any other value, and `description` unless the value is `REPLACE`. After the wrapper, it includes `behindImageStyle` for `BEHIND`.

**Version 1.** `main` writes the markup itself:

- Without a banner: `#header-inner`, with `.titlewrapper > h1.title` holding the `title` includable, then `description`.
- `BEHIND`: the same markup, with an inline `background-image` built from `sourceUrl`, `backgroundPositionStyleStr`, `widthStyleStr` and `height`. In the classic mobile view the background is left out.
- `REPLACE` and `BEFORE_DESCRIPTION`: an `img#Header1_headerimg` linked to the homepage, then `description` for `BEFORE_DESCRIPTION`. No `h1` and no title text.

### `title`

**Version 2.** An `h1` holding the title. The link to the homepage is added with a `b:tag` whose `cond` is `data:view.url != data:blog.homepageUrl`.

**Version 1.** The link only, the `h1` being in `main`. It compares `data:blog.url` with `data:blog.homepageUrl`.

### `description`

Version 2 writes the description in a `p`. Version 1 writes `.descriptionwrapper > p.description > span`.

### `image`

Includes `responsiveImage` inside `a.header-image-wrapper` linking to the homepage, with `image: data:image`, the blog title as `altText`, and `width` and `height` as `originalWidth` and `originalHeight`.

### `behindImageStyle`

When a banner is set, includes `responsiveImageStyle` with `image: data:this.image` and `selector: ".header-widget"`, which writes CSS backgrounds for several widths. Then a `<style>` with `background-position` set to `data:blog.locale.languageAlignment` and `background-repeat: no-repeat`.

The same Common includable gives a background banner to a theme's own wrapper. Blogger renders the widget as `div.Header`, so the selector can be the widget itself:

```xml
<b:includable id='behindImageStyle'>
  <b:include cond='data:image' data='{ image: data:image, selector: ".Header" }' name='responsiveImageStyle'/>
</b:includable>
```

### What Blogger's themes change

The responsive themes override three includables in their [default markups](../layout/default-markups.mdx): <BloggerSource kind="observed" />

- `title` wraps `super.title` in a `div` that gets the class `replaced` when the placement is `REPLACE`.
- `image` calls `super.image`, then also includes `title` for `REPLACE`, so the title stays in the page and is hidden with CSS.
- `description` is skipped on post pages, in Emporio only.

Contempo also adds `background-size: cover` to `behindImageStyle`.

## Writing your own markup

### A logo from the widget

When the blog owner uploads a square logo instead of a banner, the theme can print it at a fixed size. Without a saved size (`data:width` and `data:height` are `0`), [`resizeImage`](../expressions/functions.mdx) crops it to a square:

<CodeTabs>

```xml title="theme.xml"
<b:includable id='image'>
  <b:with value='data:width and data:height ? { src: data:sourceUrl, width: data:width, height: data:height } : { src: resizeImage(data:sourceUrl, 200, "1:1"), width: 200, height: 200 }' var='logo'>
    <img class='brand-logo' expr:alt='data:title' expr:height='data:logo.height' expr:src='data:logo.src' expr:width='data:logo.width'/>
  </b:with>
</b:includable>
```

```html title="Rendered, without a saved size"
<img alt='My Blog' class='brand-logo' height='200' src='https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/…/w200-h200-p-k-no-nu/logo.png' width='200'/>
```

</CodeTabs>

## Things to know

- **Types of `width` and `height`.** Strings in version 1, numbers in version 2.
- **Uploads.** A banner uploaded from the widget goes to the photo album of the blog. Set "Shrink to fit" before uploading.
- **Legacy.** `sectionWidth` and its setting are left from fixed-width themes. `mobile` and `widthStyleStr` exist in version 1 only.
